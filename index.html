The Fleuri Story

Our Story

What started in 2022 as a crochet hobby to de-stress from school quickly turned into a passion. I taught myself the basics by watching simple YouTube tutorials, spending a full year practicing and perfecting my craft.

In 2023, encouraged by a classmate who loved my work, I decided to start selling my creations. Demand grew fast, and I officially launched my own business right from my classroom using the limited resources I had.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized handmade brand known for beautiful products, excellent service, and gifts that bring lasting joy.

Our Values and Purpose

Every FLUERI bouquet is made with patience, care, and attention to detail. We value every customer, respect deadlines, and strive to make every order special.

ABOUT US

Fleuri is a premium online gift shop serving Cagayan de Oro City. We offer handcrafted floral arrangements, gourmet treats, and thoughtful gifts tailored for life’s most special occasions. Through beautiful curation, meticulous packaging, and seamless delivery, we aim to provide a five-star gifting experience—bringing happiness to your loved ones, one beautiful gift at a time.
